About GDEV

Rooted in communities. Focused on practical change.

Grassroot Development Support and Rural Enlightenment Initiative (GDEV) is a Nigerian civil society organisation whose operations began in 2004 and whose organisation was formally registered in 2018.

We work with communities, public institutions and development partners through evidence-informed programmes, local partnerships, advocacy, research and practical solutions—especially where underserved people face barriers to health, livelihoods, protection, environmental resilience and social inclusion.

Our approach combines community ownership with systems engagement, learning and accountability to help practical solutions move from local experience toward wider policy and development impact.

Our mission

Inclusive development

Advance healthier, safer, more resilient and economically empowered communities through locally grounded action and partnerships.

Evidence + community voice

What we do

Our programme areas

GDEV brings community knowledge, frontline experience and partnership-building together across interconnected development challenges.

01 • Health

Health & SRHR

Community health, sexual and reproductive health and rights, maternal health, safe abortion information, immunisation and frontline health-system support.

02 • Climate

Climate & Environment

Climate resilience, environmental action, sustainable agriculture, marine and blue-economy interests, and community adaptation.

03 • Livelihoods

Food Security & Livelihoods

Support for resilient livelihoods, agriculture, enterprise and locally appropriate economic opportunities for vulnerable communities.

04 • Protection

Protection & Social Justice

Community protection, prevention of violence and exploitation, anti-modern slavery initiatives and pathways to justice.

05 • Innovation

Digital & AI for Development

Responsible use of digital tools and practical AI-enabled approaches that strengthen frontline services and expand opportunity.

06 • Advocacy

Research & Policy

Community-informed research, evidence generation, advocacy and engagement with government and development partners.

Projects & Impact

From ideas to community action

Health systems

Community Health Innovation

Experience supporting frontline health workers, community actors and public health stakeholders through digital and practical service-delivery approaches, including responsible use of decision-support tools in low-resource settings.

Climate action

Climate-smart Communities

Work linking climate information, resilient agriculture, women’s livelihoods and locally relevant environmental action, with growing interest in plant-based agriculture, marine conservation and climate resilience.

Protection

Protection & Economic Pathways

Community-focused approaches connecting protection, youth opportunity, livelihoods, prevention, social cohesion and pathways toward safer communities.

Maternal health

Frontline Birth Preparedness

Community and frontline-provider initiatives designed to strengthen timely access to essential maternal and newborn health information, services and referral support.

Immunisation

Community Immunisation Support

Demand-generation and community engagement approaches that help families access essential vaccines and strengthen links between communities and primary health facilities.

Youth & innovation

Digital Opportunity

Practical digital and AI pathways designed to help young people apply technology to farming, trade, services, micro-enterprise and community problem-solving.
